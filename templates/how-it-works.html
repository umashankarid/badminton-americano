🏸 How Americano Works

What is Americano?

Americano is a social tournament format where everyone plays with everyone. You compete as an individual, but each match is played as doubles. Your partner changes every round, so you get to play with all other participants.

Key principles:
  • Players register individually
  • Partners rotate every round
  • Every point counts toward your personal total
  • The player with the most points wins

How Pairing Works

Round 1: Partners are assigned randomly.

Round 2+: The system ensures:

  • You partner with a different player each round (everyone partners with everyone)
  • Teams with similar points play against each other (balanced matches)
Example with 8 players and 2 courts:

Total partnerships = 28 (every possible pair). Each round uses 4 partnerships (2 matches × 2 teams). So you need 7 rounds for everyone to partner with everyone.

Sit-outs: If the number of players isn't perfectly divisible by 4 (e.g., 9 players), one player sits out each round. The system rotates who sits out fairly — whoever has sat out the most gets priority to play.

Scoring System

Each match is played to a set number of points (configured per tournament, e.g., 15 or 21). The first team to reach that score wins.

Your points = Your team's score − Opponent's score

This means:

  • Big wins give more points (winning 15-5 = +10 each)
  • Close losses don't hurt much (losing 14-15 = only −1)
  • Every point matters, even when losing!

Opponent Distribution

The system generates the entire schedule before the tournament starts, ensuring:

  • Every player partners with every other player exactly once
  • Every player faces every other player an equal number of times as opponents
  • Players rotate across courts each round
Example with 9 players: Each player faces every other player exactly 2 times as opponents across 9 rounds — perfectly fair for everyone.

Courts are automatically calculated: courts = players ÷ 4 (rounded down).
