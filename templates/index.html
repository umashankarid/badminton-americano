ℹ️ About Komet Summer Americano 2026 ▼

What is it?
Komet is proposing Americano double matches program during the summer. Americano program, as in the Komet julmästerskap and klubmästerskap, is basically getting a number of people together, and everyone plays with everyone and collect points. It will be based on 1x21 point per match. If there are 8 people, each person will play 7 sets of double with different partner.

Why do we propose this?

  • During summer, people want to train badminton and an organized program is beneficial for players, i.e. people do not need to look for partners to play with.
  • It can be expensive to buy own shuttles for matches, and not always easy to share shuttles when playing together.

When and how long?
Twice a week, Tuesday and Thursday 17:00–20:00, for 8 weeks, starting June 16th until August 6th (16 sessions total).

How much will it cost?

  • Sommarkort from Komethallen: 799 SEK (required)
  • Americano program: 700 SEK/person for full 8 weeks
  • Non-members: sommarkort 899 SEK + program 1100 SEK

With sommarkort you can also play any other time outside program hours with your own shuttles.

What do you get?
For every 10 people, 2 courts are booked with 2 dozens of shuttles available. All shuttles (used and unused) belong to BMK Komet.

What if I'm on vacation?
You can still join. If you're away for a few weeks but want to play the rest, that's fine. However, the fee remains 700 SEK (no reduced fee).

What about different playing levels?
Everyone plays with everyone at least once — no one is left out. If there are many players, we divide into groups balanced by level. After each Americano day, top 2 of Group B replace bottom 2 of Group A (promotion/relegation).

How to sign up?
Join a tournament above and register your name. You need a WhatsApp number for group communication. Sign up deadline: June 11th.

Questions?
